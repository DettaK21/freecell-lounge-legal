FreeCell Lounge Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

FreeCell Lounge is a mobile card game. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the game.

1. Game Data

FreeCell Lounge stores game statistics, progress, and preferences locally on your device. The current release does not require registration or transmit player statistics to our own servers.

2. Advertising

The game integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). When advertising services are active, Google and its partners may collect or process information such as device identifiers, IP addresses, advertising interactions, and diagnostic data, subject to their policies and applicable consent requirements.

Learn more at Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Accounts and Online Features

The current release does not provide player accounts, cloud synchronization, or online leaderboards. These features may be introduced in future versions with an updated policy.

4. Data Retention

Locally stored game data remains on your device until removed through available game controls, app data deletion, or uninstallation, depending on your device settings. Third-party advertising data is governed by the applicable provider's retention practices.

5. Children's Privacy

FreeCell Lounge does not knowingly request personal information directly from children. Advertising and age-related protections will be configured according to applicable laws and Google Play requirements.

6. Security

We aim to minimize the information handled by the game. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy as FreeCell Lounge introduces new features, advertising arrangements, or data practices. The updated version will be published at this address.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, requests, or support, contact freecelllounge@yahoo.com.
